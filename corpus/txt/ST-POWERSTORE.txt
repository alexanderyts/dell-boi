Uncompromising efficiency

As flash prices become more volatile, storage efficiency is an economic imperative. PowerStore packs more capacity,

connectivity, and compute into every rack unit, helping organizations achieve greater and more predictable value from

their infrastructure investments.

Extreme density

Industry-standard low-profile E3 NVMe media unlocks consolidation benefits legacy 2.5” formats can’t match. With up

to 40 drive slots in the base chassis alone, PowerStore accommodates up to 5.8PBe in just 3U.2

Every Gen 3 model is available with TLC or QLC media–no separate product families and no performance trade-offs.

Choose drives based on capacity and price alone.

Low-profile OCP 3.0 networking doubles connectivity with up to 40 ports per appliance. Tool-less, field-replaceable I/O

modules let you adapt quickly as protocols and speeds evolve.

Intelligent data reduction

Gen 3 models offer a guaranteed 6:1 data reduction ratio for all reducible data4, taking the guesswork out of capacity

planning. Always-on inline deduplication and hardware-accelerated compression continuously shrink the data

footprint without compromising application performance, no matter how large your environment grows.

The latest PowerStoreOS enhancements add unaligned deduplication and enhanced compression offloads leveraging

next-generation Intel QuickAssist Technology.

End-to-end cyber resilience

PowerStore keeps data secure, available, and recoverable through ransomware attacks, failures, disasters, and human

error–with protection built in, not bolted on.

Protect and detect

Cybersecurity is embedded across data, identity, operations, and compliance:

•  Data and platform security–Data-at-rest and data-in-flight encryption,9 FIPS-validated cryptography, Hardware Root of

   Trust, and Secure Boot.

•  Identity and access–MFA, SSO, Multiparty Authorization, policy-based access, secure auditing.

•  Detect and respond–Storage-layer anomaly detection, AIOps cybersecurity insights, and optional Dell Cyber Detect for

   AI–powered ransomware corruption detection with 99.99% accuracy.10

•  Standards and compliance–STIG mode, NIST-aligned controls, and an upgrade-ready architecture designed to support

   post-quantum cryptography11 and evolving regulatory requirements.

Stay available

PowerStore is designed for 99.9999% availability. Active-active controllers keep both nodes serving I/O at all times,

while the patented Dynamic Resiliency Engine (DRE) absorbs multiple simultaneous drive failures without data loss.

Hot-swappable drives, power supplies, fans, and I/O modules–along with non-disruptive software upgrades– keep

PowerStore online during routine maintenance.

Native replication over Fibre Channel or Ethernet includes synchronous, asynchronous, and metro options for block

and file. Metro Sync with 3rd-site witness provides automated failover, enabling zero-RTO and zero-RPO SLAs for any

workload at no additional cost, with configuration in as few as six clicks.12

Recover quickly

Immutable Secure Snapshots and File-Level Retention help preserve trusted recovery points against ransomware,

accidental deletion, and unauthorized change.

PowerStore Storage Direct Protection sends backups directly from the array to PowerProtect Data Domain with up to

75:1 deduplication13—without a backup server or host impact. Integration with PowerProtect Data Manager adds fleet-

wide orchestration, while PowerProtect Cyber Recovery provides an isolated, immutable vault. Dell Cyber Detect helps

identify the last known clean copy on PowerStore for fast, confident recovery.

AI-powered simplicity

PowerStore takes routine work off administrators at every layer—with a platform that tunes and optimizes itself, AI

insights that surface what matters, and agents that act on policy across the fleet. Together, these capabilities form the

industry’s most advanced automation ecosystem for primary storage, reducing manual effort by up to 95%.5

Self-optimizing by design

Dynamic Node Affinity and intelligent load balancing distribute workloads across nodes and appliances. Dynamic

Resiliency Engine automates drive-level protection, and always-on data reduction continuously improves capacity

efficiency–all without manual tuning.

Smart Support analyzes telemetry from Dell’s global install base to identify emerging failure patterns. PowerStore’s

onboard health engine uses those insights to proactively address up to 79% of predicted issues without manual

intervention.14

AI-driven insight

Dell AIOps delivers predictive analytics, guided remediation, capacity forecasting, and cybersecurity insights,

accessible from any device. A built-in AI assistant combines Dell’s knowledge base with local system telemetry to

answer questions in natural language, saving administrators up to eight hours per week.15

Agentic operations

Dell AIOps agents automate fleet-wide lifecycle tasks, including PowerStoreOS updates and policy-based capacity

reclamation. Routine work can run autonomously while administrators retain control of lifecycle decisions.
