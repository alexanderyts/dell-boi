                      Specifications

                      Scale up and Scale out

                      PowerMax is built from modular storage components for compute and storage media.

                      The compute modules are packaged as node pairs. Each node pair contains two

                      PowerMax compute nodes, complete software and licensing, cache memory,

                      redundant power, and connectivity modules. These are combined with 48-slot

                      Dynamic Media Enclosures (DMEs) to configure NVMe flash drives. PowerMax

                      arrays are delivered with the Inclusive Software package. NVMe drive capacity can

                      be added to the system to scale up to a total effective capacity of 8 PBe on the

                      PowerMax 2500 and up to 18 PBe on the PowerMax 8500.
