                                       Important Notes

•  Original NDR/400GbE (100G-PAM4-based) transceivers support both InfiniBand and Ethernet depending on the switch inserted into.

•  New Ethernet-only 400GbE (100G-PAM4-based) transceivers are offered (legacy part numbers with “-T” at the end).

•  400GbE QSFP112/MPO-APC Single mode 500m transceiver now available. No 2-fiber LC version offered.

•  No 400G FR4 QSFP112 or OSFP (100G-PAM4-based) transceivers offered.

•  100G-PAM4 cables and transceivers cannot downshift to 50G-PAM4 or 25G-NRZ. Use DACs and AOCs splitters.

•  Use 400G transceivers with 1:2 fiber cable splitters for 200G rates (2 fibers activates 2 channels & reduces power)(no 200G parts offered).

New Terms

•  IHS = Integrated Heat Sink = Finned top (for switches-only)

•  RHS = Riding Heat Sink = Flat top (for CX7/BF3, DGX-H100/200 and liquid-cooled systems

QSFP-DD for SN5400/SN4700 Switches

•  Only QSFP-DD offering is 400G DR4 for SN5400/SN4700 switches.

•  QSFP-DD cages on SN5400/SN4700 switches are backwards compatible with QSFP56 and QSFP28 devices (but don’t support QSFP112)

•  QSFP112 cages on CX7 and BF3 support backwards compatibility with QSFP56 and QSFP28 devices

200GbE/HDR

•  Separate 200GbE and HDR portfolio part numbers merged to one set of HDR/200GbE part numbers.
