· Original NDR/400GbE (100G-PAM4-based) transceivers support both InfiniBand and Ethernet depending on the switch inserted into. · New Ethernet-only 400GbE (100G-PAM4-based) transceivers are offered (legacy part numbers with "-T" at the end). · 400GbE QSFP112/MPO-APC Single mode 500m transceiver now available. No 2-fiber LC version offered. · No 400G FR4 QSFP112 or OSFP (100G-PAM4-based) transceivers offered.
· 100G-PAM4 cables and transceivers cannot downshift to 50G-PAM4 or 25G-NRZ. Use DACs and AOCs splitters. · Use 400G transceivers with 1:2 fiber cable splitters for 200G rates (2 fibers activates 2 channels & reduces power)(no 200G parts offered).

Quantum-2 & SN5600 ONLY Accept Twin-Port, IHS Finned-Top Devices

Additional cooling fins integrated on top of transceivers

Used in Quantum2 and SN5600 switches only with switches using reverse air flow configuration

Open Finned Top (IHS) (DACs, ACCs, Transceivers)

All Twin-port devices for air-cooled switches use Finned-Tops.
DGX H100s Cedar7 GPU cages only accepts Twin-port devices --- with flat tops (-FLT in part number)
Switch cages and DGX cages have different OSFP heights and are not interchangeable (e.g. Flat will not work in Finned cage) Air-cooled switches can accept open and closed Finned top devices as they are the same height

Finned-top devices are only used in switches 8-channel, 2x400G devices (Tcvrs, DACs, ACCs)
Everything else uses a flat-top devices (Tcvrs, DACs, ACCs) for CX7 and BF3, HDR
